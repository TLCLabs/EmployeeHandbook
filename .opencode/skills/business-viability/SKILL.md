# Business Viability

Help test whether an idea can support a compassionate, durable business.
Follow AGENTS.md. Provide analysis and questions, not finished business plans,
financial policies, or professional financial advice.

## Workflow

1. Read manifesto.md at the repository root and relevant company material.
   Establish the current revenue model and commitments before advising.
2. Clarify the decision, time horizon, operating stage, and material constraints.
   Ask for the minimum information needed; anonymized or approximate figures
   are acceptable. Never invent company financial data.
3. Separate supplied figures, sourced facts, estimates, and assumptions. Show
   units, time periods, and calculations so the user can check the reasoning.
4. Examine the relevant economics, rather than forcing every topic into a
   full financial model:
   - Pricing and per-unit materials, assembly labor, packaging, and fulfillment.
   - Warranty, repairs, returns, support, and replacement-parts obligations.
   - Fair pay, benefits, paid rest, overhead, and realistic production capacity.
   - Inventory purchases, payment timing, working capital, and cash reserves.
   - Demand uncertainty, supplier risk, and durability of revenue.
5. Distinguish revenue, gross margin, operating profit, and available cash.
   Include founder labor or identify its exclusion. Avoid presenting an idea
   as profitable merely because sale price exceeds component costs.
6. Use a few scenarios or break-even estimates when useful. Explain which
   assumptions most affect the result and avoid false precision.
7. Suggest practical ways to improve viability and a small test or missing
   piece of evidence. Keep final decisions with the user.

## Company Constraints

Respect the documented no-subscriptions commitment and revenue streams of
pre-assembled devices, self-assembly kits, and standalone hardware components.
Identify alternative revenue proposals as departures, not adopted strategy.

Include the employee commitments in cost and capacity discussions: the 10:1
pay ratio, 32-hour work week with 40-hour pay, and vacation. If affordability
is uncertain, explain the gap and options without defaulting to benefit cuts
or unpaid work.

The manifesto's "5 more years, every year" mindset is a longevity goal, not
evidence of an existing five-year cash reserve.

## Guardrails

Flag assumptions needing current local evidence or professional validation,
especially Saskatchewan employment, tax, registration, and regulatory matters.
Use credible, current sources if external research is needed. Do not imply
B Corp certification from the company's described social-enterprise model.

Do not request unnecessary sensitive records, promise outcomes, modify files,
or produce publication-ready documentation.
